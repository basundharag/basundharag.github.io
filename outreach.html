Sharing the Universe

I love talking about the Universe with anyone who is curious about it, whether in a café, a classroom or a school science fair. Here are some of the ways I try to share science beyond research papers.

Public talks

Apr 2026The Universe is Expanding and Why it is a Big Deal
Pint of View Club, Maize and Malt, Bengaluru: the accelerating expansion of the Universe, and how recent observations are making us question what we know about dark energy. Audience of about 160. Event page
Feb 2026The Universe is Tricking You: Why Galaxies Don't Look the Way They Are
Astronomy on Tap, Green Door Café, Bengaluru: how gravitational lensing turns the cosmos into a funhouse mirror, and how we use it to map invisible dark matter. Astronomy on Tap
